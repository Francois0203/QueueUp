        Connect Spotify

        One quick setup

        Spotify asks every DJ to create a free personal "app key". It takes about 3 minutes, and you only do it once — this device remembers it.

        • A Spotify Premium account (needed for queueing)
        • Your Spotify login — QueueUp never sees your password
        • Spotify open on the device that plays the music

        Create your key

        Open Spotify's developer site, log in, and fill in the form with these values. Tap to copy.

        First time on Spotify's developer site?

        Spotify asks you to accept its developer terms and may ask you to verify your email before you can create an app. That's normal — do that, then come back here.

        Paste your Client ID

        On your new app's page, copy the Client ID. Or just copy the page's web address — we'll find the ID in it.

        It's 32 letters and numbers. You don't need the client secret.

        Finish this step on the computer running QueueUp: when testing locally, Spotify can only send you back to 127.0.0.1.

        Spotify shows an error?

        "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI" — the Redirect URI in your Spotify app doesn't match. Go back one step, copy it again, and paste it in your app under Settings → Edit → Redirect URIs. Then Save.

        "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client" — the Client ID is wrong. Copy it again from your app's Settings.

        Can't find your app? Open developer.spotify.com/dashboard and click it.
