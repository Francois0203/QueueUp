        Connect Spotify

        Needs Spotify Premium. You connect through your own free Spotify developer app — a one-time, 3-minute setup. QueueUp never sees your Spotify password.

        1. Open the Spotify Developer Dashboard, log in, and click Create app.
        2. Give it any name and description. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly:
        3. Tick Web API, accept the terms and Save.
        4. Open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID and paste it below. You don't need the client secret.

        Finish this step on the computer running QueueUp: Spotify only returns you to 127.0.0.1 when testing locally.
