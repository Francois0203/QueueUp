Privacy Policy

Version {{TERMS_VERSION}}

The short version. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, and we never sell data. We keep only what a party needs to work, and delete it automatically. Played and dismissed songs go after 3 hours, and whole parties go 48 hours after they're last used, or right away when the host ends them.

1. Who is responsible

{{OPERATOR_NAME}}, {{OPERATOR_ADDRESS}}, is responsible for your personal data on QueueUp (the "controller"). Contact: {{OPERATOR_CONTACT}}.

2. What we collect

If you're a guest

  • A random, anonymous sign-in ID created by Firebase Authentication and kept in your browser's storage. It's how we count your votes once and apply request limits. It isn't linked to your identity.
  • Your display name, only if you type one. Others at the party may see it next to your requests if the host shows names.
  • Your song requests, searches and votes for that party.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly in memory to protect the service, for example to stop people guessing party codes. QueueUp doesn't store it, though our hosting provider may keep standard network logs.

If you're a host

  • Your party name, settings, DJ PIN, and which anonymous sign-in IDs may control your DJ console.
  • A record that you accepted the Terms (which version, and when).
  • If you connect Spotify: your Spotify developer app's Client ID, access and refresh tokens, your Spotify display name and subscription type (used to warn you if queueing needs Premium), and what's currently playing.

3. Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To run your party: show requests, count votes, queue songs and show what's playing. This is necessary to provide the service you asked for.
  • To keep QueueUp safe: rate limits, blocking abuse and preventing attacks. This is our legitimate interest in a secure service.
  • To keep a record of the Terms a host accepted: our legitimate interest in being able to show that agreement.

We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions.

We don't use cookies. We only store what's strictly necessary in your browser for QueueUp to work, so we don't show a consent banner.

5. Who we share it with

  • Other people at the party see requests, vote counts and, if the host allows it, names. The host sees all requests.
  • Spotify, if the host connects it: searches and queue requests go to Spotify through the host's connection. Album artwork is loaded straight from Spotify's servers by your browser, so Spotify receives your IP address when artwork is shown. Spotify's use of data is covered by Spotify's Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider ({{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}), which runs QueueUp's website, database, sign-in and server functions for us as a data processor, under Google's data processing terms. Google may log technical data such as IP addresses to operate and secure its services.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it.

We don't sell personal data or share it for advertising.

6. International transfers

Our servers are with {{HOSTING_PROVIDER}}. Spotify operates internationally. Where data leaves your country, we rely on the safeguards the law requires, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

  • Played and dismissed requests: deleted after 3 hours.
  • Whole parties, including requests, names, the PIN and any Spotify tokens: deleted 48 hours after the last activity, or immediately when the host ends the party.
  • Disconnecting Spotify deletes the Spotify tokens straight away.
  • IP-based security data: held in memory for at most 1 hour, never written to disk by QueueUp.

8. Security

Stored data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), Spotify access tokens are never sent to browsers, and database rules allow browsers to read only what each person may see, and we keep as little as possible. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and will tell you, and the relevant authorities, about a breach where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, POPIA or US state laws, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your data protection authority.

You can act on many of these yourself, instantly. Guests can withdraw their requests. Hosts can end a party, which deletes everything, and can disconnect Spotify or revoke access at spotify.com/account/apps. For anything else, email {{OPERATOR_CONTACT}}. QueueUp has no accounts, so we may ask for the party code and the name you used, to find your data.

10. Children

QueueUp isn't intended for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the version date above, and we'll make material changes clear on the site.
